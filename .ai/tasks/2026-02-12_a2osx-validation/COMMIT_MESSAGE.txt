Blocking Issues Identified:
1. BUG-001 (P0 CRITICAL): Symbols fail in addressing modes
   - Symbols only work with # prefix (immediate mode)
   - Cannot use zero page, absolute, or indexed addressing
   - Blocks 99% of real-world 6502 assembly

2. BUG-002 (P1 HIGH): .BS directive misinterpreted  
   - Currently treats as binary string (0/1 only)
   - Should reserve N bytes of space (block storage)
   - Affects 280 usages in A2oSX (2.9% of directives)

3. BUG-003 (P1 HIGH): Expression operators not supported
   - Missing: < (low byte), > (high byte), + (offset)
   - Required for pointer manipulation and 16-bit addressing
   - Affects ~550 expressions in A2oSX (5.7% of code)
